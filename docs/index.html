Better predictions are
only the beginning.

Making a chip means coordinating processes, tools and factory resources. A better prediction matters when it leads to a better decision.

Our survey follows that connection across five manufacturing scopes and nineteen tasks: what can AI observe, what should happen next, and how can feedback improve the outcome?

The vision

An AI-native
autonomous virtual fab.

Models predict the consequences of actions. Agents coordinate decisions across tasks. Physical systems provide the feedback to improve them.

Together, they form a digital environment that connects isolated predictions to coordinated factory decisions.
